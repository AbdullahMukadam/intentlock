Intentlock is run by Abdullah Mukadam, an individual based in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we handle and why. Contact: [support@intentlock.space](mailto:support@intentlock.space).

## The software you install

Intentlock is self-hosted. Everything it stores (users, plans, decisions, audit logs) stays in your own database on your own servers. The software does not send us any data, and license keys are checked offline.

## This website

The site uses no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. It stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser only. The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes basic request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect it.

## When you buy a license

Checkout is run by [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com), our merchant of record. They collect your payment and billing details under their own privacy policy; we never see your card details.

Dodo Payments sends us the details we need to issue your license, and we keep them:

- your name, email address and, if you give one, your company name;
- your subscription: seat count, status and billing dates;
- every license key issued to you, and whether its email was delivered.

We use this only to issue and email your license keys, to send renewal and seat-change keys, to answer support requests and to keep financial records.

## "Resend my license" requests

When you ask us to resend a key, we record the email address you entered and your IP address for one day, to limit how often keys can be requested. Then they are deleted.

## Who processes data for us

- **Dodo Payments**: payments, tax and invoices.
- **Cloudflare**: hosting the website and the service that issues keys, including its database.
- **Brevo**: sending license emails.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

License and subscription records are kept for as long as your subscription exists and for as long as tax and accounting rules require afterwards. Resend requests are deleted after one day.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, where the law allows. Email [support@intentlock.space](mailto:support@intentlock.space). Deleting your records means we can no longer resend or renew your keys.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
