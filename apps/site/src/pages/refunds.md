**All payments for Intentlock licenses are final and non-refundable**, except where the law requires a refund.

## Try before you buy

Intentlock runs in a free mode with every feature, limited to 5 users, 3 agents and 30 days of audit history shown. You can install it and use it fully before paying, so please make sure it fits your needs before you buy a license.

## Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically each year. To avoid being charged for the next year, cancel before the renewal date. Renewal charges are not refunded.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your current key keeps working until its expiry date. You are not charged again and you do not get a partial refund for the rest of the year.

## Added seats

Seats added during a year are charged pro rata and are not refunded.

## Billing mistakes

If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, email [support@intentlock.space](mailto:support@intentlock.space) from the address you bought with and we will correct it.

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record.
